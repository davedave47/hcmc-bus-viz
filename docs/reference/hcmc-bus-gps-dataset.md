## **Abstract** 

This document describes a bus Global Positioning System (GPS) trajectory dataset from the Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam public bus network. The dataset is prepared specifically for educational and research purposes. All sensitive and identifiable information has been anonymized and encoded to ensure data privacy and ethical use. 

The dataset captures real-world, large-scale, streaming like GPS messages generated by buses operating in Ho Chi Minh City. It is designed to practice data ingestion, preprocessing, large-scale data analysis, and exploratory analytics using big data tools and frameworks. 

**Keywords:** GPS, bus trajectory 

## **1 Background: Ho Chi Minh City Bus Network** 

Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC) operates one of the largest urban bus networks in Vietnam, serving hundreds of thousands of passengers daily. The bus system is operated and coordinated under the supervision of the Ho Chi Minh City Management Centre of Public Transport (MCPT), which is responsible for planning, managing, and regulating public bus services across the city. The network consists of: 

- A dense set of inner-city routes. 

- Suburban and inter-district routes. 

1 

- Multiple types of terminals and stations (terminals, transfer points, regular stops). 

The bus system plays a critical role in urban mobility and is currently being integrated with new public transport infrastructure, including the metro system. As a result, the bus network provides a rich and realistic data source for transportation analytics and big data research.

## **3 Time Period of Data Collection** 

The dataset covers a specific and continuous **50-day** time window, from **March 20 to May 10, 2025** . This period was deliberately selected to capture a wide range of operational and traffic conditions in HCMC, allowing for robust temporal and comparative analysis. The selected time window includes: 

- Regular working days, reflecting typical weekday traffic demand and commuting patterns. 

- Weekends, which often exhibit different mobility behaviors, service frequencies, and congestion levels. 

- Major public holidays and special events, including: Hung Kings Commemoration Day (07/04); Reunification Day (30/04); International Workers’ Day (01/05); The April 30th military parade period (24/04–30/04).

## **5 Dataset Size and Storage** 

The complete dataset is hosted on **Kaggle** and on the **HPC Lab shared file system** (sharefile.hpcc.vn) ( _links will be provided separately_ ). After decompression, the dataset occupies approximately **34GB** , reflecting the high temporal resolution and large volume of GPS messages collected over the 50-day period, necessitating the use of high-performance data processing techniques such as partitioning, sampling, or distributed computing frameworks. 

To facilitate initial schema verification and exploratory analysis, a representative sample dataset is provided. This sample file mirrors the structural integrity of the primary dataset while remaining computationally lightweight for rapid inspection. 

## **6 Dataset Highlights and Special Notes** 

This dataset contains several characteristics that distinguish it from simplified or synthetic transportation datasets. You should carefully consider the following highlights and special conditions when conducting analysis: 

- Public holidays and special events: Travel behavior, traffic conditions, and bus operating patterns during public holidays differ significantly from regular weekdays. Reduced demand, altered service frequencies, and atypical congestion patterns may be observed. 

- Large-scale event disruptions: Periods such as the April 30th military parade introduce non-recurring disruptions, including road closures, traffic control measures, and temporary rerouting. These events create clear structural breaks in trajectory patterns and travel times and should be explicitly modeled rather than treated as noise. 

- Irregular sampling and missing data: As with most real-world GPS-based datasets, sampling intervals may be inconsistent, and certain records may be missing due to communication loss or operational issues. Robust preprocessing and data quality assessment are therefore essential. 

- Route-specific operational behaviors: Different bus routes exhibit distinct characteristics depending on their geographic coverage, service type, and traffic environment. Inner-city routes, suburban routes, and long-distance routes may show markedly different speed profiles, dwell times, and reliability levels. 

- Bus routes connected to metro lines: Selected routes serve as feeders or connectors to metro stations. These routes may demonstrate unique temporal patterns, peak-hour behavior, and passenger demand dynamics associated with intermodal transfers.

Taken together, these characteristics make the dataset particularly suitable for advanced big data tasks, including anomaly detection, spatio-temporal pattern mining, travel time modeling under non-stationary conditions, and comparative analysis between normal and disrupted periods. 

## **7 Intended Use** 

This dataset is primarily intended for academic research in transportation analytics and urban mobility in the context of HCMC, with a strong emphasis on large-scale data-driven methods. It is designed to support both methodological development and empirical analysis using real-world GPS trajectory data derived from the HCMC public bus network, which operates under complex and dynamic urban traffic conditions.
