\section{Design Artefacts}
\label{sec:prototype:artifacts}

In addition to the executable EDA notebooks, the UI mockup, and
the warehouse skeleton, Phase~1 produced two further design
artefacts that the Phase~2 implementation depends on.

\paragraph{The project log (\texttt{CLAUDE.md}).}
A single-file project log (approximately 350 lines) records
every locked design decision from the brainstorming phase. Each
decision is annotated with its section in this report; where
the report and the log diverge, the log is the authoritative
source of truth (the log is updated in lockstep with every
locked decision, while the report is regenerated less
frequently). The log also enumerates the four EDA notebooks
catalogued in Section~\ref{sec:prototype:eda}, the seven
reference works that ground the design
(Knaflic~\cite{knaflic2015}, Liu~\cite{liu2022},
Srisakaokul~\textit{et~al.}~\cite{srisakaokul2023},
Derevitskiy~\textit{et~al.}~\cite{derevitskiy2016},
Lin~\&~Zeng~\cite{linzeng2014}, the HCMC dataset
specification~\cite{hcmcgps2025}, and the advisor's analytics
checklist~\cite{thoai2025}), and the running brainstorm record.

\paragraph{The brainstorm corpus.}
A directory of HTML files under
\texttt{.superpowers/brainstorm/.../content/} documents the
iterative UI explorations that converged on the v8 design
shown in Section~\ref{sec:prototype:mockups}. The corpus is
preserved as a design trail and is referenced from
Section~\ref{sec:future:roadmap} as evidence that the visual
language chosen here is the product of structured iteration
rather than first-attempt fixation.

Together with the report itself, the UI mockup, the four EDA
notebooks, and the warehouse skeleton, these artefacts compose
the complete Phase~1 deliverable set. The four Phase~1
deliverables required by the capstone topic
description~\cite{capstoneDescription} ---
\emph{(i)~studying the HCMC bus dataset}
(Section~\ref{sec:prototype:eda}),
\emph{(ii)~proposing related analytical problems}
(Section~\ref{sec:proposed:problem}),
\emph{(iii)~designing the analysis and visualisation solution}
(Chapters~\ref{chapter:proposed} and~\ref{chapter:prototype}),
\emph{(iv)~researching and selecting the appropriate techniques
and tools}
(Chapter~\ref{chapter:background} and
Sections~\ref{sec:proposed:warehouse_alts},
\ref{sec:proposed:module_rationale},
\ref{sec:proposed:dashboard_alts}) ---
are therefore complete. The Phase~1 deliverable
\emph{``Phát triển, thử nghiệm và đánh giá ban đầu''}
(``develop, test, and conduct initial evaluation'') is partially
met by the warehouse skeleton and the four executed notebooks;
full Phase~2 development continues per the roadmap in
Chapter~\ref{chapter:future}.
