\section{Scope of the Phase-One Study}
\label{sec:intro:scope}

The study covers two operational data streams of the Ho Chi Minh City
bus network. The AFC stream covers the 90-day window from 1 January
2026 to 31 March 2026 inclusive and contains approximately 17.9
million boarding events with paired payment records. The GPS stream
covers the 50-day window from 20 March 2025 to 10 May 2025; the
present project loads a single-day sample (18 March 2025) of
approximately 720,000 pings for Phase One, with the full 50-day window
loaded in Phase Two. The static reference tables (4,710 stops, the
operating companies, and the schedule and route master data) are
loaded in full. The two operational streams do not share any calendar
day; the implications of this mismatch are discussed in
Section~\ref{sec:solution:dataset:coverage}.

Six classes of analytical question are in scope, organised into six
analytical themes covered in Chapter~\ref{chapter:solution}: demand
and crowding patterns; school-aware demand patterns specific to
university and school commuting; spatial and connectivity questions
including bus-to-metro feeders; financial and operational efficiency;
network-wide health and reliability; and per-route deep analysis with
cross-route comparison. The themes serve four user roles internally
but are not exposed to the user through a role selector.

Phase One produces the present report, four executed exploratory
analysis notebooks documenting the dataset and grounding the design,
a high-fidelity user-interface mockup that locks the visual
specification of the proposed system, and a partial implementation of
the data-preprocessing pipeline at Layer 1 of the proposed
architecture.

The following capabilities are explicitly out of scope and are not
addressed by the proposed system.

\begin{itemize}
\item Real-time monitoring (the proposed system is historical-only).
\item Per-passenger journey reconstruction (the AFC user identifier
is populated for approximately 0.1 percent of records, which is
insufficient for individual analysis).
\item Exact financial modelling (no operational cost data is
available; revenue can be reported but cost-side analysis is
excluded).
\item Natural-language query (a separate team is developing that
capability).
\item Live vehicle occupancy at a given moment (the AFC stream
records boardings only, not alighting events, so occupancy is not
computable from the available data).
\end{itemize}
