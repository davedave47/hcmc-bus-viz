\section{Outline}
\label{sec:intro:outline}

The remainder of this report is organized as follows.
Chapter~\ref{chapter:related} surveys the existing tools for the Ho
Chi Minh City bus network and the prior research on bus operational
analytics and analytical visualization, and identifies the gap the
project addresses. Chapter~\ref{chapter:foundations} introduces the
theoretical and technical concepts the proposed solution depends on,
including the Multiple Coordinated Views (MCV) interaction model that
underpins the platform's cross-chart linking.
Chapter~\ref{chapter:design} develops the proposed solution across
problem analysis, dataset characterization, preprocessing,
architecture, analytical themes, visualization technique selection,
and interaction model. Chapter~\ref{chapter:implementation} documents
the Phase One implementation artefacts and the initial evaluation of
the platform against the stated requirements.
Chapter~\ref{chapter:conclusion} sets out the Phase Two implementation
roadmap, the deployment plan, the planned user evaluation, and the
predictive and analytical extensions that follow the historical
foundation laid in Phase One. Chapter~\ref{chapter:conclusion}
synthesizes the work and records its contributions and limitations.
