\section{Full GPS Dataset Activation}
\label{sec:future:gps}

The most immediate data activation is the upload of the full
fifty-day GPS dataset of approximately thirty-four gigabytes
referenced in the dataset
specification~\cite{hcmcgps2025}. The warehouse architecture
specified in Section~\ref{sec:proposed:dataarch} is designed
to scale to this data volume without code change: the raw views
are parquet glob patterns that natively absorb new daily files;
the fact views are non-materialised and therefore self-update;
and the eight aggregate tables are re-built with
\texttt{CREATE OR REPLACE TABLE} statements that complete in
under five minutes on commodity hardware.

The activation unlocks seven charts that the local one-day
sample leaves under-powered. The seven are catalogued in the
data-availability milestones of
Section~\ref{sec:proposed:milestones}:

\begin{itemize}
\item 3.4 Effective vs ghost stops (door-open ratio on the full
    fifty-day window stabilises the twelve-percent ghost-stop
    finding).
\item 3.5 Bus--Metro connectivity (the arrival-time-matching
    matrix requires headway statistics that the one-day sample
    cannot supply).
\item 5.4 Reliability scorecard (QoS-1/2/3 on the full sample
    rather than the nine-route extracted buffer).
\item 6.1 Route constellation (the UMAP feature vector
    \texttt{agg\_route\_profile} includes GPS-derived
    reliability features).
\item 6.2 Archetype intersection matrix (depends on the
    archetype labels in \texttt{dim\_route}, which the UMAP
    job in P9 backfills).
\item 6.5 Marey time--distance diagram (one route at a time;
    requires multiple days of GPS to render meaningful
    bunching patterns).
\item 6.6 Stop-by-stop boardings + door dwell (combines AFC
    boardings with GPS door-open ratios; requires the full GPS
    sample for the door-open denominator).
\end{itemize}

The April~30 parade week, mentioned in
Section~\ref{sec:proposed:eda_to_design} as a natural-experiment
case study, also becomes fully analysable once the May~2025
GPS data is in. The GroupSet temporal clustering mentioned in
Section~\ref{sec:background:related} lifts from a planned
visualisation to an actually-rendered one: the seven-day-by-hour
demand patterns the GroupSet partitions require fifty days of
GPS to populate reliably.

The activation is scheduled at the start of P9
(Section~\ref{sec:future:roadmap}). If the Kaggle pull
completes earlier, the activation moves forward and the
SHOULD-tier charts above demote to MUST-tier on the timeline.
