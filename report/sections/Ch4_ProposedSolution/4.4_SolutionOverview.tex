\section{Solution Overview}
\label{sec:solution:overview}

The proposed system is an analytical platform over the preprocessed Ho Chi
Minh City bus data. The system is organised around four design components
that compose into the analytical surface visible to the user. First, a
layered analytical warehouse sits over the preprocessed data and produces a
fixed set of aggregate tables that the surface reads from. Second, the
analytical content is grouped into a fixed catalogue of six thematic views,
each of which addresses one class of analytical question and reads from one
cluster of aggregate tables. Third, each visualisation in the catalogue is
matched to a single primitive chart type selected to fit the question the
chart answers, with rejected alternatives recorded as part of the design
record. Fourth, an interaction model connects the visualisations across each
thematic view and provides a uniform response to user clicks on analytical
entities.

The remaining sections of this chapter develop the four components in turn.
Section~\ref{sec:solution:architecture} describes the data architecture and
records the architectural alternatives considered.
Section~\ref{sec:solution:themes} catalogues the six thematic views, their
target user roles, and the analytical questions they answer.
Section~\ref{sec:solution:viz} records the chart-to-primitive mapping per
theme, with rejected alternatives where relevant.
Section~\ref{sec:solution:interaction} specifies the three interaction
patterns the surface adopts from the literature on coordinated views and on
visual storytelling.

The implementation tools that realise each component are introduced in the
foundations chapter (Chapter~\ref{chapter:foundations}) and are not named in
this section, in order to keep the present discussion focused on the design
decisions rather than the realisation technology.

The chapter as a whole follows a four-step argument that mirrors the
order in which a system designer reasons. Section~\ref{sec:solution:problem}
establishes the problem, the users, the analytical needs, and the
functional and non-functional requirements. Section~\ref{sec:solution:dataset}
describes the data sources available to address those needs and reports
the exploratory observations that constrain the design.
Section~\ref{sec:solution:preprocessing} reports the pipeline that
transforms the raw input into a queryable layer. The four design-component
sections that follow (architecture, themes, visualisations, interactions)
draw on the existing tools, research literature, and visualisation
guidance surveyed in Chapter~\ref{chapter:related} and
Chapter~\ref{chapter:foundations}, and translate the requirements of
Section~\ref{sec:solution:problem} into the specific design responses
recorded in each section.
